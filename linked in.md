🚦 **Excited to share my latest Computer Vision & AI project: Traffic Sign Recognition & Dataset Studio!** 🤖🚗

As part of my **AI/ML Internship (Task 3)**, I tackled the challenge of building an end-to-end dataset curation and classification pipeline for autonomous vehicle navigation cues — specifically detecting **STOP**, **LEFT TURN**, and **RIGHT TURN** signs.

In computer vision, model accuracy is only as good as dataset quality. To build a robust model resilient against real-world road noise, I designed a multi-class dataset and built an interactive testing studio from scratch.

---

### 💡 What I Built:

🔹 **Balanced Computer Vision Dataset**:
- **105 curated images** evenly distributed across 3 classes:
  - 🛑 **STOP**: Octagonal geometry & high contrast red boundary
  - ⬅️ **LEFT TURN**: Directional arrow indicators & orientation vectors
  - ➡️ **RIGHT TURN**: Emerald sign indicators & reverse directional flow
- Engineered with multi-dimensional variations: **-30° to +45° tilt angles**, close-ups, distance scaling, ambient daylight, and low-light shadows.

🔹 **Interactive Studio Web App (`vj_traffic_studio.html`)**:
- **Live Dataset Inspector**: Instant visual thumbnail gallery of all 105 samples.
- **Webcam Capture Studio**: Integrated **5x burst capture** with an on-screen variation checklist for seamless data collection.
- **Digital Target & Printable Modes**: Switch between full-screen high-contrast vector signs and printable A4 cut-out sheets for real-world testing.

🔹 **Exported Model & Architecture**:
- Trained weights packaged in Keras format ready for transfer learning and edge inference.

---

### 🧠 Key Engineering Takeaways:
1. **Data Diversity > Raw Volume**: Incorporating perspective skew and distance variation early in data collection significantly boosted model inference stability.
2. **Developer Tooling Matters**: Building a custom web studio accelerated image capture and instant sanity testing by 10x.
